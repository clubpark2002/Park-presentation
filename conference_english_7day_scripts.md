# Conference English: 7-Day Audio Scripts

안녕하십니까. 오늘 참석해 주셔서 감사합니다.
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for joining us today.

My name is Youngjin, and I work at the National Disaster Management Institute in Korea.
저는 한국의 National Disaster Management Institute에서 근무하고 있는 영진입니다.
My name is Youngjin, and I work at the National Disaster Management Institute in Korea.

My research focuses on disaster safety technology, spatial information, and satellite imagery.
저의 연구 분야는 재난안전 기술, 공간정보, 위성영상입니다.
My research focuses on disaster safety technology, spatial information, and satellite imagery.

Today, I’d like to present our recent research on satellite-based disaster monitoring.
오늘은 위성 기반 재난 모니터링에 관한 최근 연구를 발표하겠습니다.
Today, I’d like to present our recent research on satellite-based disaster monitoring.

I’ll briefly cover the background, methodology, results, and future directions.
연구 배경, 방법론, 결과, 향후 방향을 간략히 말씀드리겠습니다.
I’ll briefly cover the background, methodology, results, and future directions.

Let’s begin with the background and motivation for this study.
먼저 연구 배경과 필요성부터 살펴보겠습니다.
Let’s begin with the background and motivation for this study.

## Day 2 — Background and Problem Statement
Welcome to Day 2. Today we will explain why the research is necessary.

As you may know, extreme weather events are becoming more frequent and severe.
아시다시피 극한기상 현상이 더욱 빈번하고 심각해지고 있습니다.
As you may know, extreme weather events are becoming more frequent and severe.

These events can cause significant damage to people, infrastructure, and local communities.
이러한 재난은 인명과 기반시설, 지역사회에 큰 피해를 줄 수 있습니다.
These events can cause significant damage to people, infrastructure, and local communities.

One of the key challenges is the lack of timely and reliable information.
주요 문제 중 하나는 신속하고 신뢰할 수 있는 정보가 부족하다는 점입니다.
One of the key challenges is the lack of timely and reliable information.

Conventional methods are often limited by delays in data collection and analysis.
기존 방식은 데이터 수집과 분석에 시간이 걸린다는 한계가 있습니다.
Conventional methods are often limited by delays in data collection and analysis.

This makes it difficult for decision-makers to respond quickly during an emergency.
이 때문에 재난 상황에서 의사결정자가 신속하게 대응하기 어렵습니다.
This makes it difficult for decision-makers to respond quickly during an emergency.

The question we wanted to address was how to detect risks earlier and support faster decision-making.
저희는 위험을 조기에 탐지하고 의사결정을 신속하게 지원하는 방법을 찾고자 했습니다.
The question we wanted to address was how to detect risks earlier and support faster decision-making.

## Day 3 — Objectives and Technology Overview
Welcome to Day 3. Today we will practice explaining research objectives and the technology.

The main objective of this project is to improve the accuracy and timeliness of disaster response.
이 과제의 주요 목적은 재난 대응의 정확성과 신속성을 향상하는 것입니다.
The main objective of this project is to improve the accuracy and timeliness of disaster response.

Our approach combines satellite imagery, GIS, and field-based observations.
저희 방법론은 위성영상, GIS, 현장 관측자료를 결합합니다.
Our approach combines satellite imagery, GIS, and field-based observations.

The system is designed to process data in near real time.
이 시스템은 데이터를 준실시간으로 처리하도록 설계되었습니다.
The system is designed to process data in near real time.

We developed a model to identify areas vulnerable to flooding and landslides.
침수와 산사태 취약지역을 식별하는 모델을 개발했습니다.
We developed a model to identify areas vulnerable to flooding and landslides.

The system is intended to support evidence-based disaster management.
이 시스템은 근거 기반 재난관리를 지원하기 위한 것입니다.
The system is intended to support evidence-based disaster management.

In practical terms, the goal is to provide useful information before and during an emergency.
실무적으로는 재난 전과 재난 중에 유용한 정보를 제공하는 것이 목표입니다.
In practical terms, the goal is to provide useful information before and during an emergency.

## Day 4 — Methodology and Data
Welcome to Day 4. Today we will practice explaining data and research procedures.

The workflow consists of four main steps.
전체 처리 과정은 네 가지 주요 단계로 구성됩니다.
The workflow consists of four main steps.

First, we collect satellite images and environmental data.
첫째, 위성영상과 환경 데이터를 수집합니다.
First, we collect satellite images and environmental data.

Second, we preprocess the data and remove noise and missing values.
둘째, 데이터를 전처리하고 잡음과 결측값을 제거합니다.
Second, we preprocess the data and remove noise and missing values.

Third, we extract key indicators, including rainfall, elevation, slope, and land use.
셋째, 강우량, 고도, 경사도, 토지이용 등 주요 지표를 추출합니다.
Third, we extract key indicators, including rainfall, elevation, slope, and land use.

Finally, we run the model and validate the results using historical disaster data.
마지막으로 모델을 실행하고 과거 재난 데이터로 결과를 검증합니다.
Finally, we run the model and validate the results using historical disaster data.

This figure shows how the data flow through the system.
이 그림은 데이터가 시스템에서 어떻게 처리되는지를 보여줍니다.
This figure shows how the data flow through the system.
